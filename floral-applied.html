Counting it up

Across the whole site that is the swag on the section dividers, the sprig on two headings per page, and the corner sprays on one dark band per page. Three placements, one of them replacing something that already existed.

The reason to stop here is that flowers read as intentional when they are rare and as a theme when they are everywhere. Toggle them off and on a few times: the version with flowers should look like the same site with better manners, not like a different site.
