# Your website editor

Go to **admin.blessyourpawspuppies.com** on your phone or computer and tap **Google** to sign in
with your Gmail account. If that ever gives you trouble, type your email address instead and
Cloudflare emails you a code. You stay signed in for 30 days.

Nothing you change shows on the website until you press **Publish**. Then the site updates in about
two minutes.

## When a puppy is reserved or adopted

A family who pays the deposit or the full price online does this for you. The puppy is marked
Reserved or Adopted on the site within a few minutes, you get an email with a link to the payment
in Stripe, and **History** shows the change from **stripe**. A balance payment changes nothing.

For a payment taken by phone or in cash:

1. Open **Puppies**.
2. Tap **Reserved** or **Adopted** beside the puppy.
3. Open **Publish** and tap **Publish to the site**.

The site takes the price and the deposit buttons off that puppy for you.

## To add photos of a puppy

1. In **Puppies**, tap the puppy's name.
2. Tap **Add photos** and choose them from your phone.
3. Tap **Save**, then publish.

The first photo is the cover on the cards and the puppy's page. Use the arrows to move a photo
earlier or later, and **Hide** to take it off the puppy's page. Hidden photos stay in the gallery,
and photos are never deleted.

## When a new litter is born

1. Open **Litters** and tap **Start a new litter**.
2. Choose the mom and dad, then enter the birth date and the expected size. The prices start at
   the last litter's, so change them only if this litter's price is different.
3. In **Puppies**, tap **Add a puppy to this litter** for each puppy.
4. Add a photo to each puppy, then publish.

A puppy shows on the site once it has a photo.

## To change the price or the deposit

Open the litter in **Litters**, change **Price**, **Cash price** or **Deposit**, tap **Save**, then
publish. Use whole dollars before sales tax. Every puppy in the litter and the online payments
change together.

To price one puppy differently, open the puppy in **Puppies** and fill in both **Price for this
puppy** and **Cash price for this puppy**, then save and publish. Clear both to go back to the
litter's prices. The deposit is always the litter's.

## To change the litter photo

Open the litter in **Litters**, tap **Replace the photo**, describe the photo in a few words, tap
**Save**, then publish. It shows at the top of the puppies page. The old photo is kept.

## The size note

The note under each puppy's expected size names the parents and their weights. It reads the
**Weight** line on each parent's card in **Parents**, so update the weight there.

## When every puppy in a litter is adopted

Open the litter in **Litters** and tap **Move to past litters**, then publish. Its puppy pages come
off the site, and links to them go to the puppies page. Its photos stay in the gallery.

## Reviews

Open **Reviews** and tap **Add a review**. Type it word for word, as the family wrote it.

## If something looks wrong

The **Publish** screen lists every change waiting to go on the site, and **Undo** puts one back to
how it is on the site now. If you are stuck, call or text Alex.
