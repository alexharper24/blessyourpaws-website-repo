Our dogs, and what they are tested for

Health claims are easy to make and hard to check. So each dog gets their own testing listed here, and where a record exists we link the record itself and put a QR beside it. Scan it with your phone and read the original.

Mini Multi Gen Bernedoodle

Troy

Troy is the mother of our Munchkin Bernedoodle litter. At 22 lbs she is a small, easygoing girl with a blue merle parti coat, and she passes on both the size and the temperament we breed for. Sample copy, waiting on their words

Weight: 22 lbs, blue merle parti, born January 21, 2024.

Genetic testing: Wisdom Panel, tested February 21, 2026. Clear on 29 conditions. Carries one copy of the chondrodystrophy variant, CDDY. It takes only one copy to matter, so it can pass to a puppy. The report is linked below and the panel explains it on pages 4 and 5.

The report: linked here, with every health result the panel returned.

Every puppy, before go-home

What happens before a puppy leaves us

  • Examination by a licensed veterinarian
  • Age-appropriate vaccinations and deworming, records in the go-home folder
  • Microchipped
  • Early neurological stimulation from the first weeks
  • A written health guarantee
