Making the mobile hero land

Four treatments at a real 390px. The dashed line marks the top of his head.

The two things you asked for pull against each other. Bringing the puppy down means putting empty space above him, which makes the photo smaller and quieter. Making it stand out means giving it more of the screen. The dashed line on each mock is measured live from the image as it is actually drawn, so the numbers below are real rather than my estimate.

The trade comes out like this. A gives the most air and the least presence, roughly 60px above his head in a frame about 250px tall. B, C and D give the most presence and about 20 to 28px of air, in photos 350 to 470px tall. They get away with less air because they are full bleed: there is no border for him to bump against, which was the actual complaint. A puppy 24px below the edge of the screen reads completely differently from one 14px below a hard green frame line.

One useful property of B, C and D: all three are taller than the photo's own 3:2 shape, so they crop the sides and show the full height of the picture. Nothing can get cut off his head or his paws in any of them, at any screen width.

A Mounted print Live now

What is on the site at this moment. A 4:3 frame around a 3:2 photo, the puppy anchored to the bottom, and the gap above him filled in the paper colour so it reads as a mount. It solves the head-bumping cleanly. It does not make the photo feel important, and the empty band can read as a mistake if you are not expecting it.

Two things to decide

A correction worth recording, since it changed my own advice: I first wrote that option B bought 88px of air. It buys about 24. I had assumed a tall box crops the top of the photograph, when in fact it crops the sides and shows the full height. The markers on this page are measured from the rendered image for exactly that reason.

Which treatment, and whether to keep this photograph. They are separate questions. Every option above gets more out of the picture than the site does today, but all four are still working around a photo with 5.4% headroom. If you want to look at alternatives, the five with the most room to spare are griffin-01, malcolm-03, malcolm-01, jericho-09 and jordan-04. The first three are Joy's Dobermans, so using one would change the hero from Hope's actively selling litter to Joy's, which is a business call rather than a design one.

Desktop is untouched by all of this. The drift treatment stays exactly as it is above 900px.
