Floral placements, seven candidates

Same five source files throughout. Everything here uses the real stylesheet, so what you see is what would ship.

Hope asked for flowers. The risk is a greeting card, so these are ordered from hardest to get wrong to most likely to tip into twee. My recommendation is at the bottom.

1 Swag as the section divider Safest

The site already draws a small sage sprig between sections. This swaps that one decorative element for the real thing and adds nothing new. It is the entire motif for the price of one asset, it never touches the copy, and it reads as stationery rather than decoration.

2 Sprig beside a heading Safest

A single bloom at 44 to 62px next to a section heading. Small enough to be punctuation, and it gives the florals a job on every page without a single large placement. Use it on two or three headings per page at most.

What I would ship

Options 1, 2 and 6-restrained, and nothing else. The swag replaces the existing divider so the motif appears on every page without adding a single element. The sprig accents two headings per page. One dark band per page gets the corner sprays at low opacity, which is the only placement with real visual impact and the only one that uses the pinks the way the palette intends.

That is three placements. The reason to stop there is that florals read as intentional when they are rare and as a theme when they are everywhere, and a theme is what makes a breeder site look amateur. Option 3 is worth one page if Hope wants more. I would skip 4, 5 and 7.
