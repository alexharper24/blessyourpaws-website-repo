How it works

From first hello to go-home day

Five steps, no pressure, and a real conversation somewhere in the middle. Here is exactly what happens, so nothing about buying a puppy from us is a surprise.

1

Say hello

Browse the available puppies, then call, text, or send the inquiry form. Tell us a little about your family and who caught your eye. There is no application fee and no pressure.

Most families text a photo of the puppy they like and go from there. See who is available.

2

Meet the puppy

We set up a visit or a video call so you can meet the puppy, meet the parents, and meet us. Ten minutes is usually enough to know. We would rather talk you out of the wrong puppy than sell you one.

Visits are by appointment. Our exact location is shared once your visit is booked.

3

Reserve with a deposit

A $500 deposit holds your puppy while they finish growing up with us. It applies to your balance, and it is transferable to another available puppy if your plans change.

Refund terms are being finalised. Draft, confirm before launch

4

Watch them grow

We send photos and updates right up to go-home day, and you can ask for more any time. Plenty of families end up on a first-name basis with us before they ever pick the puppy up.

Ask us anything in between. We would rather over-communicate.

5

Go-home day

Puppies go home from eight weeks, after their final vet check. You leave with the health records, the paperwork, and a puppy who already knows what a family sounds like.

Pickup is by appointment. Delivery options are being finalised. Draft, confirm before launch

In the go-home bag

What comes home with your puppy

Every puppy leaves with their paperwork, their food, and something that smells like home.

Every puppy goes home with

  • Vaccination and health record
  • Examination by our vet
  • Small bag of the food they know
  • Collar and leash
  • A toy

Every puppy comes with a written health guarantee.

Before you ask

The questions we get most

How do payments work? The $500 deposit reserves your puppy online. The balance is due before or at pickup, and most families pay it by check or bank transfer. Draft, confirm before launch

Can we visit first? Yes, and we encourage it. Video calls work well for families further away.

Will my puppy shed? It varies by puppy, even in one litter. We never promise a non-shedding coat. More on coats.
