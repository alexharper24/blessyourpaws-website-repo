About us

Two sisters, one standard

We are Hope and Joy, twin sisters raising puppies in our northern Indiana homes. Every litter is raised the same way: in the house, around our kids, in the middle of everything. Sample copy, waiting on their words

We grew up in a big family where there was always something cooking and someone at the door, and our puppies grow up the same way. By the time a puppy leaves us it has heard the vacuum, the doorbell, and a houseful of children, and it has been held every single day.

Why we do it this way

A puppy's first eight weeks decide a lot about the dog they become. That is why ours are never raised apart from the household. They meet children, other dogs, the vacuum, and visitors before they ever meet you. Sample copy, waiting on their words

We would love for you to meet the puppies, and their parents, before you decide. Visits are by appointment, and video calls work well for families further away.
