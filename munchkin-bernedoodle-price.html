How much is a Munchkin Bernedoodle?

A Munchkin Bernedoodle from our litter has an adoption fee of $2,060. Here is exactly what that covers, how the deposit and the cash price work, and what else to plan for once your puppy is home.

  • Adoption fee$2,060
  • Indiana sales tax7%, added to the total
  • Paid in full by card$2,204.20 with tax
  • Cash adoption fee$2,000
  • Deposit to reserve$500
  • Balance dueBefore or at pickup
  • We acceptCard or cash, not checks

The $2,000 cash fee applies only when the deposit and the balance are both paid in cash.

How the deposit works

A short application comes before the deposit. The $500 deposit then holds your puppy while they finish growing up with us, and it comes off what you owe at pickup. The deposit is non-refundable. If your plans change, it can move to another available puppy.

The whole sequence, from first visit to going home, is on our how it works page.

Included in the fee

What comes home with your puppy

The adoption fee covers the puppy and everything below. Color, sex and coat do not change it, so every puppy in the litter carries the same fee.

Every puppy goes home with

  • Vaccination and health record
  • Examination by our vet
  • Microchipped
  • Signed health guarantee
  • Small bag of the food they know
  • Collar and leash
  • A blanket
  • Toys

Delivery is not included. If you would like your puppy brought to you, you arrange it and pay for it directly with our delivery partner.

What to budget for afterward

The adoption fee is the largest single cost, but it is not the last one. Plan for food, vaccinations and routine vet care, and professional grooming every six to eight weeks, since a doodle coat needs it. Our page on shedding and grooming explains why.

Costs vary by area, so ask a local vet and groomer for their prices before your puppy comes home.
